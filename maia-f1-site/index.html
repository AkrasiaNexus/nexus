Sidepods & floor

Invisible grip. Relentless downforce.

Sidepods curve around the wheels to limit the Magnus effect that wheel rotation creates. The tail tapers so the air reattaches cleanly.

Two curved rear-wing sections meet at the centre to form one continuous surface. Air stays attached for longer.
