01 · The envelope

Every millimetre, inside the rules.

The chassis was modelled from the regulated no-go zone, keeping a strict 30 mm offset for full compliance with the design rules.

02 · Front wing

Air goes around, not through.

Two ramped front wings deflect air away from the wheels. Chord 25 mm, thickness 10 mm, span 69.04 mm.

03 · Side pods

No room for the Magnus effect.

Smooth side pods curve around the wheels to control airflow and reduce turbulence, limiting the Magnus effect generated by wheel rotation.

04 · Rear

One cartridge of CO₂.

The CO₂ cartridge chamber is 18 mm wide and 52 mm deep. The rear pods are hollowed outside the no-go zone to cut mass legally.
