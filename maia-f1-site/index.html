01 The reveal

Engineered for the limit.

Team MAIA Racing builds its F1 in Schools car inside a strict envelope. The model you are looking at is the regulated no-go zone itself: every part of the car lives inside it.

  • 227mm envelope length
  • 74mm width
  • 30mm offset from the no-go zone

02 Aerodynamics & front wing

Sculpting every molecule of air.

A pair of curved air guiders sits beneath and around the chassis. The edges steer air away from the wheels, cutting turbulence at the front of the car and balancing it at speed.

Airflow streamlines shown are illustrative.

03 Monocoque, halo & cockpit

Built around the driver.

The halo platform and cockpit lift clear of the survival cell. The chassis is streamlined so the body stays clean through the air while the driver is held in place.

04 Sidepods, floor & venturi

Invisible grip. Relentless downforce.

Sidepods curve around the wheels to control airflow and limit the Magnus effect that wheel rotation creates. The rear section tapers cleanly so the air reattaches smoothly.

05 Rear wing & running gear

Precision kinematics at speed.

Two curved rear-wing sections meet at the centre to form one continuous surface. Air stays attached for longer, which cuts pressure drag and the wake behind the car. The rear flap moves on the scrub.

Scrub back and forth to actuate the rear flap.
