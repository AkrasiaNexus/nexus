    All spots

    Every route starts at Abu Dhabi Bus Station. The first 10 spots use Google Maps transit data for ITC buses (10 Nov 2026); the rest use DARB line lists from Moovit (20 Nov 2026), with estimated trip times. A few spots have no direct bus and are priced as a shared cab. Check a route the first time you take it.

    Fares are estimates using Hafilat's AED 2 + 5 fils per km, capped at AED 5 a trip. Taxi estimates use Abu Dhabi's metered fare from admobility.gov.ae: AED 5 flag fall + AED 1.82/km, AED 12 minimum, 4 people per cab. Spot hours and prices are from wow-abudhabi.com and digiflow.ae (both checked Sept 2026). Your map and Lore Book save in this browser only.
